# Healara India website

Static site for Healara, Korean microalgae and fucoxanthin skincare. Product facts, prices and clinical results come from the official Healara India brochure.

- `index.html`, `styles.css`, `app.js`: the site (no build step)
- `data.js`: the product catalogue, rituals and concerns. Edit prices or products here.
- `assets/`: product cutouts, clinical study strips and imagery extracted from the brochure

Orders are sent to the Healara India team on WhatsApp (+91 91544 61422).
