    Why it works

    Two actives grown from Korean sea microalgae, explained in plain words. Both are named after Dr. Cheol-Ho Pan, who first isolated fucoxanthin in pure form in 2012.

    panFucoxanthin

    What it does for you: shields skin from light and inflammation, two big triggers of pigmentation and early ageing.

    A natural carotenoid from brown algae and marine microalgae like diatoms. It can’t easily be synthesised, so it’s sourced from nature. It absorbs visible light between 400 and 500 nm and is known for strong antioxidant, anti-inflammatory and anti-ageing effects.

    In the Firming Ampoule (750 ppm) and Activating Cream (450 ppm).

    panChlorella vulgaris

    What it does for you: calms irritated skin and feeds it proteins, vitamins and minerals so it recovers faster.

    A microscopic green microalga, 2 to 10 μm across, that makes proteins, vitamins, minerals and polyunsaturated fatty acids through photosynthesis. It’s a powerful antioxidant with anti-inflammatory properties that help calm the skin.

    In all nine formulas, from 0.015% to 0.03%.

    Questions

    What is fucoxanthin, and why is it in skincare?

    Fucoxanthin is a natural carotenoid found in brown algae and marine microalgae. It absorbs visible light between 400 and 500 nm and is valued for its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory and anti-ageing effects. Healara’s panFucoxanthin is in the Core Lift Firming Ampoule and the Core Revive Activating Cream.

    Where is Healara made?

    Healara is developed and made in Gangneung, South Korea, from 15 years of research at KIST’s Gangneung Institute of Natural Products and Microalgae Ask Us. It’s imported to India by Amorisha Empires LLP.

    Is Healara suitable for sensitive skin?

    The range is certified for all skin types and dermatologically tested on humans. In clinical testing, the UV Serum Moisture scored 0.00 on a sensitive-skin irritation test, and no adverse reactions were seen with the Barrier Protect Cream. Patch-test any new product first.

    Is it vegan and cruelty-free?

    Yes. Healara is 100% vegan and not tested on animals.
